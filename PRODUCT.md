# Speculib

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
TypeScript and React inside Electron. Three.js renders the library space. PDF.js reads and extracts PDFs. Windows is the current development environment.

## Users
People managing their personal PDF library and looking for relevant books, chapters, and passages.

## Product Purpose
Browse subject shelves or ask a librarian in natural language. Open whole resources at page one and individual matches at their physical PDF pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Keep PDF import, local storage, text extraction, retrieval, resource editing, fiction/nonfiction filters, reader, and independent collapsible navigation and librarian panels. Resources can have several subjects. Answers must use only library evidence. Semantic embeddings and OCR remain pending. Optional Ollama models now provide a tool-driven librarian and separate evidence-based categorization; users choose installed local models independently.

## Brand Commitments
Dark, space-inspired desktop interface. The user's supplied references show colored UMAP-like topic clusters, restrained sci-fi HUD surfaces, and a deep-space atmosphere. The entire library stays visible in the background as light points. Explore mode allows 3D flight and reveals covers on approach. Approximately 20/60/20 functional columns, each side independently collapsible. No slogans, decorative introductions, fake coordinates, redundant headings, or workspace breadcrumbs. The user rejected the incumbent card dashboard and authorized replacing its visual design.

## Evidence on Hand
User references are attached in the conversation and available on the Desktop. Real PDFs remain local. Empty-library sample covers are explicitly marked previews and never answer sources. Existing product requirements are in APP_PLAN.md.

## Product Principles
- Find sources accurately and preserve their page context.
- Keep browsing and reading controls concise and discoverable.
- Let the library space remain visible behind the working interface.
- Distinguish real content and capabilities from illustrative previews.
